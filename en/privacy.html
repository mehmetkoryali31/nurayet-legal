Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Version 1

For users in Türkiye, the KVKK Information Notice is section 14 of this policy.

1. Data controller

Nurayet is developed by Mohammed Ibrahim Nooruldeen. Data controller: Mohammed Ibrahim Nooruldeen. Contact: mehmetkoryali31@gmail.com.

2. No account

There is no sign-up or login. The app does not ask for your identity, phone number, email, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

3. Stored only on this device

The following is stored only on this device and is never sent to us:

  • Age group: on first launch you are asked for your birth month and year; your birth date is not stored, only your age group (under 13, 13–17, 18 and over) and the month the group will change, which updates automatically over time.
  • Preferences: language, colour, appearance (general or Kids' Section), chosen reciter, notification preferences and notification sound.
  • Usage: where you left off, bookmarks, favorites, learning progress, downloaded audio.
  • Prayer times: the city you choose or, if you tap "Use my location", your location coordinates; the calculation method and the times calculated from them.
  • Kids' Section: surahs marked as memorized.
  • Records about these documents: which version you accepted and when, and your answer to the personalised-ads option and when you gave it.

4. Ads

Nurayet is currently completely ad-free: the ad software is not run and no data is sent to Google for advertising. If ads are added in the future, the rules below apply; this text will be updated and shown to you again in the app.

If ads are added, they are served by Google (Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ads appear only in permitted places (e.g. home and explore cards, after finishing a learning level). Ads are never shown while reading or listening to the Quran, in translation, tafsir or hadith texts, with prayer-time notifications or on the qibla screen. Access to religious knowledge is never locked behind ads.

To serve and measure ads and prevent abuse, Google processes: IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version), your interactions with ads (views, taps, watch time), ad service diagnostics and - only on iPhone and only if you allow it - the advertising identifier (IDFA). This data goes directly from Google's own software to Google, not from the app; it does not reach us as personal data. Google processes it under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Until you tap "Accept and start" on the welcome screen, the ad software is not started and nothing is sent to Google.

5. Personalised (interest-based) ads

By default ads are not personalised. Interest-based ads are shown only if you turn on the separate, unticked option on the welcome screen or in Profile > Privacy & ads. This option is for ages 18 and over. On iPhone, turning it on asks Apple's tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) once; if you decline, ads are not personalised. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is also shown and your answer there is applied. You can withdraw at any time in Profile > Privacy & ads; withdrawing is as easy as giving. The app works the same without it.

6. Age and ads

The ad software does not start until your age group is known. If you are under 13, the ad software never starts and no ads are shown. If you are 13–17, ads are never personalised, Google is told you are under the age of consent and a teen user, and iPhone tracking permission is not asked. Personalised ads are only for ages 18 and over. Ad content is limited to a general-audience level. The age group entered on this device can later only be lowered. Choosing the kids discovery appearance does not change your age group.

7. Location and country

You can choose your city manually for prayer times and qibla; location permission is not required. If you grant it, your location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and qibla and is not sent to us. To apply ad rules, the app reads your store account's country (or else the device region) on the device; this never leaves the device.

8. Audio, hadith and content downloads

When you listen to or download Quran audio, your device connects to the server hosting it (mp3quran.net); when you open hadiths, it connects to the hadith server (hadeethenc.com). These servers see your IP address as a normal part of any internet connection. No personal information is sent with these connections. Hadiths you open are stored on the device so they can be read offline.

9. Notifications

Prayer-time and other reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; they are off by default and only sent if you turn them on. No data is sent to us for notifications.

10. Not present at this time

The app has no analytics or crash-reporting system of our own, no cloud save, no chat or messaging and no in-app purchases.

11. International transfers

The ad service (Google) may process data outside Türkiye, including in the United States. Details for users in Türkiye are in section 14.

12. Deletion, withdrawal and your rights

In Profile you can delete your progress, downloaded audio and preferences from this device. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data on the device. You can withdraw personalised-ads consent in Profile > Privacy & ads and turn off the advertising identifier in your device settings. Depending on the law of your country you have rights to access, correct, delete, object and withdraw consent. Requests: mehmetkoryali31@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Changes

If this text changes, it is shown again in the app and the effective date is updated.

14. For users in Türkiye: KVKK Information Notice

This notice is given under Article 10 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"). Explicit consent for personalised ads is obtained separately and optionally (14.4 and 14.5 below) and only for ages 18 and over. No ads are shown under 13; ads are not personalised for ages 13–17.

14.1 Data controller

Data controller: Mohammed Ibrahim Nooruldeen. Nurayet is developed by Mohammed Ibrahim Nooruldeen. Contact: mehmetkoryali31@gmail.com.

14.2 Data processed and method of collection

a) Data that stays only on your device: your age group (birth month and year are asked, birth date is not stored), preferences, progress, bookmarks, downloaded content, chosen city, the acceptance record of these documents and your answer to the ads option. You enter these in the app or they arise as you use it. They are not sent to us and the data controller has no access to them.

b) Ad data: automatically collected by Google's ad software (Google AdMob) when ads are shown: IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics and - only on iPhone and only if you allow it - the advertising identifier (IDFA).

14.3 Purposes

Running the app on your device and keeping your preferences; showing ads so the app can be offered free, measuring ads and preventing ad fraud; showing interest-based ads only with your explicit consent.

14.4 Legal grounds

Data on your device: performance of the contract with you for using the app (KVKK Art. 5/2-c). Showing and measuring non-personalised ads and preventing abuse: the data controller's legitimate interest, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (KVKK Art. 5/2-f). Interest-based (personalised) ads: your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1). Consent is optional; without it you can use the app the same way. The app does not collect special-category data about religious belief; if a feature such as worship tracking is added it will be off by default, stay only on your device, and this notice will be updated.

14.5 International transfers

Ad data is processed by Google LLC (USA) and its affiliates outside Türkiye, including in the United States. The transfer is made under KVKK Art. 9: for non-personalised ads within the appropriate safeguards in Art. 9/4 (standard contract announced by the Board); for personalised ads additionally on your explicit consent. The optional choice on the welcome screen is explicit consent for interest-based ads and the international transfer made for them. Data on your device is not transferred anywhere.

14.6 Retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not store ad data; Google keeps ad data for limited periods under its own rules (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

14.7 Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw personalised-ads consent at any time in Profile > Privacy & ads. New ads are then not personalised; processing before withdrawal is not affected.

14.8 Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, request information if so, learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly, know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad, request correction if incomplete or inaccurate, request deletion or destruction where the conditions are met and notification of these actions to the third parties, object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage due to unlawful processing. Send requests by email to mehmetkoryali31@gmail.com, stating your name, contact details and request. Requests are concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If your request is rejected, the answer is insufficient or not given in time, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.
